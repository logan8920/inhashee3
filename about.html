Our Story

Why we started Inhashee

We kept hearing the same thing from stylists and clients alike: a great in-salon treatment loses its shine within weeks because the home-care routine that follows it isn't built for the same hair.

Inhashee was built to close that gap — treatments and home-care formulas designed to work together, not against each other, so the results you paid for at the salon actually last.
